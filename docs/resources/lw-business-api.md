Business registration, ownership, and financial state. Separate from the physical world presence of a business, which is handled by `lw-businesses`.

## Purpose

- Business registration and ownership reads
- Business financial state (revenue, expenses, treasury)
- Ownership transfer between characters and organizations
- Business type and status management
